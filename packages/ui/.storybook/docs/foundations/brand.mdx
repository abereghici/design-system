# Brand

## The Logo

**Stâlpul cerului**, the pillar of the sky. In Romanian folklore a column holds up the sky, and Brâncuși's _Endless Column_ grew out of the carved pillars of village houses. Here the column is a zigzag, the old sign for lightning, held between two angles: the link between ground and sky. A developer also reads it as `< / >`.

It is drawn on a 32-unit grid with straight cuts, square caps and miter joins. Its size, clear space and color rules live on the [Logo](?path=/docs/components-images-and-icons-logo--docs) page.

## Personality

Moldovan and Romanian folk motifs, the stitched signs of the _ie_ and the carved signs of wood, applied as finishing touches. Every motif does a job, and none is figurative.

| Motif                                                              | Where it appears                                                                                                     |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| The **stave**, a single carved upright, as on a shepherd's _răboj_ | The Spinner's eight turning staves                                                                                   |
| The **bob** (_ochi_), the stitched diamond                         | The selected radio, single-choice menu items, the dots in the more, info and warning icons, the Separator's ornament |
| **Carved geometry**: straight cuts and 45° chamfers                | Every icon, including the octagonal search, info and danger glyphs; checks have square ends                          |
| **Moss and lichen**                                                | The brand green of the primary action, and the moss-tinted shadows                                                   |

Use one motif per surface at most, and keep them out of copy, names and illustrations.
