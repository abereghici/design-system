Adds three hues, `palette-lake-*` (slate blue), `palette-spruce-*` (teal) and `palette-heather-*` (mauve), from 50 to 950 at the weight of `clay` and `fern`, for syntax highlighting and terminal colors. The DTCG source files are now published as `@abereghici/tokens/source/<file>.tokens.json`, for tools that resolve the tokens themselves.
