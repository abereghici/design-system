Controls are the same size with a mouse and on a touch screen: the `size-control-*` tokens no longer grow under `(pointer: coarse)`. Instead, on a touch screen, a Button, IconButton or ToggleButton takes presses in an invisible area above and below itself, up to `size-target-recommended` tall.
