Add `Stepper`: the steps of a multi-step flow as an ordered list, with the current step marked for assistive technology, completed steps named by an author-written `completedLabel`, links back to completed steps through `asChild`, and a horizontal or vertical `orientation`. It is a Server Component, and `@abereghici/tokens/components/stepper.css` holds its tokens.
