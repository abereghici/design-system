Add `Splitter`: two panels and the handle between them, which people drag, or move with the arrow keys, Home and End, and collapse with Enter. The handle is a focusable separator that announces its position, its range and the panel it resizes. The size is a Change request (`value`, `defaultValue`, `onValueChange`), Enter dispatches `ab-toggle`, and `orientation` stacks the panels. `@abereghici/tokens/components/splitter.css` holds its tokens.
